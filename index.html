ABOUT GUPTA CONSULTANCY

Professional support, in language you understand.

Gupta Consultancy is an accounts and tax consultancy based in Bhayandar, Maharashtra. We aim to help individuals, professionals and businesses manage accounting records, tax documentation and routine compliance requirements in an organised way.

Our approach is straightforward: understand the requirement, agree on the scope, keep information organised and communicate clear next steps.
